Event Strategy

Working with sponsors without turning the day into an advert

By the L1C team · 14 Jul 2026 · 4 min read

← All articles

Sponsors fund a lot of good events, and they are right to expect something in return. Problems start when that return is a series of product pitches the audience did not ask for.

A better approach is to agree early what each sponsor can offer the guests: a practical session, a roundtable they host, or an introduction to someone relevant. Visibility follows from being useful, and sponsors tend to renew when their name is linked with a session people enjoyed.

Put the rules in writing before the event, including speaking time, branding and any follow-up with guest details, so everyone knows where the lines are.
