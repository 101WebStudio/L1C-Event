What makes a keynote worth sitting through

By the L1C team · 28 Jul 2026 · 4 min read

← All articles

The best keynotes make one argument and stick to it. The speaker knows what they want the room to think differently about, and every story and slide serves that point. If a talk can be summed up in a single sentence, it is usually a good one.

Length matters too. Twenty tight minutes followed by real questions will beat forty minutes of material nobody asked for. Brief speakers on the audience, the time slot and what the previous session covered, so they do not repeat each other.

As the host, protect the questions. A moderator who has read the talk in advance and prepared three good questions makes the speaker, and the session, better.
